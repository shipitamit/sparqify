Initial commit: sparqix, a stdlib-only converter to Apache Spark

Converts pandas, Polars, Dask, R, SAS, notebooks, dbt, Excel, Scala/Java,
plain Python, 40 SQL dialects, stored procedures and ETL tools (Informatica,
Talend, Teradata BTEQ/TPT, ...) to PySpark or Spark SQL.

Code it cannot translate safely is left as TODO comments. Includes a
verify loop, tests and a real-Spark CI job.
